# Digital Gift Cards

Digital gift cards are delivered via email immediately following purchase.

## Policy & Rules:
- Available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $250.
- Gift cards never expire and carry no inactivity fees.
- Non-refundable and cannot be redeemed for cash unless required by law.
- Gift card codes can be applied during checkout in the "Gift Card / Promo Code" box.
