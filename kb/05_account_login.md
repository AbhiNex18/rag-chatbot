# Account & Login Help

Managing your customer account and security settings.

## Password Reset:
- Click "Forgot Password" on the Login page.
- Enter your registered email address to receive a secure 6-digit verification code or password reset link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

## Account Lockout:
- Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.

## Updating Profile Information:
- Log in and navigate to "Account Settings" to update email addresses, phone numbers, or default shipping destinations.
- To delete your account and personal data, submit a privacy request through Customer Service.
