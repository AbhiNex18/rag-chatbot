# Return Policy

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return window for all unused, unworn items in their original packaging with tags attached.

## Key Return Rules:
- **Return Window**: Requests must be submitted within 30 days of the delivery date.
- **Condition**: Items must be unwashed, unworn, and unaltered. Clearance items labeled "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
- **Refund Method**: Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the return.
- **Return Shipping Fee**: Standard return shipping is free for store credit returns. A $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from refunds issued to the original payment method.
- **How to Initiate**: Log into your account, visit "Order History", select the order and click "Request Return" to generate a prepaid shipping label.
