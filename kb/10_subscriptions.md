# Subscription Management

Manage recurring product subscriptions, delivery schedules, and payment preferences.

## Features:
- Save 10% on all subscription items.
- Choose delivery intervals of 30, 60, or 90 days.

## Subscription Rules:
- **Skip or Delay**: You can skip an upcoming shipment up to 24 hours prior to the renewal date via "My Subscriptions".
- **Cancellation**: Cancel anytime without penalty after your first recurring shipment.
