# Order Cancellation & Modifications

Orders enter our automated fulfillment pipeline immediately after placement.

## Cancellation Window:
- **30-Minute Window**: Orders can be self-cancelled within 30 minutes of purchase from the "Order Details" page in your customer portal.
- **After 30 Minutes**: Once processing begins, orders cannot be modified or cancelled. You can return the shipment once received following our standard Return Policy.

## Address Corrections:
Shipping address updates are only guaranteed within the 30-minute grace period.
