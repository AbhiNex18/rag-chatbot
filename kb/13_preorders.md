# Pre-Orders & Product Releases

Pre-orders allow you to reserve upcoming items before official release.

## Pre-Order Terms:
- **Billing**: Payment is authorized and collected at checkout to secure your reservation.
- **Estimated Ship Date**: Displayed on the product page and confirmation email. Ship dates are subject to minor manufacturer delays.
- **Cancellation**: Pre-orders can be cancelled for a full refund anytime prior to shipment notification.
