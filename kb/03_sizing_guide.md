# Sizing Guide & Fit Tips

Our apparel is designed following standard US sizing conventions.

## Apparel Size Chart (Inches):
- **Small (S)**: Chest 34-36", Waist 28-30", Hips 34-36"
- **Medium (M)**: Chest 38-40", Waist 32-34", Hips 38-40"
- **Large (L)**: Chest 42-44", Waist 36-38", Hips 42-44"
- **Extra Large (XL)**: Chest 46-48", Waist 40-42", Hips 46-48"

## Footwear Sizing:
- Shoes run true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend ordering half a size up for athletic footwear.

## How to Measure:
- **Chest**: Measure around the fullest part of your chest under your arms.
- **Waist**: Measure around your natural waistline.
