# Damaged or Defective Items

If your package arrives damaged in transit or items are missing/defective, we will replace them immediately at no cost.

## Reporting Steps:
1. Contact support within 7 days of package delivery.
2. Provide your order number (e.g., ORD-1001), tracking number, and photos of the damaged box/product.
3. Upon verification, a replacement order will be dispatched with expedited shipping, and a prepaid return label will be provided if return of the damaged item is required.
