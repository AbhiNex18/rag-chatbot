# International Shipping & Customs Duties

Information for international orders and duty assessments.

## Customs & VAT:
- International orders may be subject to local import taxes, duties, and customs brokerage fees levied by the destination country.
- Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for select countries (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid). Where DDP is unavailable, the recipient is responsible for duties upon delivery (DDU).
- We do not misdeclare package values or mark shipments as "Gifts".
