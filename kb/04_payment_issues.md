# Payment Issues & Accepted Payment Methods

We accept major credit/debit cards and digital wallet payment options.

## Accepted Methods:
- Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay
- Store Gift Cards and Store Credit

## Common Payment Errors & Troubleshooting:
- **Card Declined**: Verify billing address matches your card issuer file, check expiration date, and confirm security CVV code.
- **Multiple Pending Charges**: If an attempt failed due to billing zip code mismatch, your bank may display a temporary pre-authorization hold that automatically drops in 24-48 hours.
- **Currency**: All transactions are billed in USD. Foreign transaction fees may apply depending on your issuing bank.
