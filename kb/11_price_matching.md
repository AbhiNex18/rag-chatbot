# Price Matching Policy

We match identical items offered at lower prices by authorized US retailers.

## Eligibility Conditions:
- Item must be identical in brand, color, size, and model year.
- Must be currently in stock at both our store and the competitor's website.
- Request must be submitted within 14 days of purchase.
- Excludes auction sites, marketplace third-party sellers, flash sales, and clearance liquidations.
