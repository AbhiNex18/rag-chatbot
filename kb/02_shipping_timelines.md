# Shipping Timelines and Delivery Methods

We process orders Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed before 12:00 PM EST ship the same business day.

## Shipping Options:
- **Standard Shipping (US Continental)**: 3 - 5 business days ($4.99 or free on orders over $50).
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2 business days ($14.99).
- **Next-Day Air**: 1 business day ($24.99).
- **International Shipping**: 7 - 14 business days depending on destination customs clearance ($19.99 flat rate).

## Order Tracking:
Once your package leaves our fulfillment center, you will receive an email confirmation containing your tracking number and carrier link (UPS/FedEx/USPS).
