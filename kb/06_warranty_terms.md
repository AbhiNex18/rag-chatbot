# Warranty Terms & Coverage

We provide a 1-year Limited Warranty on all non-perishable hardware, gear, and apparel against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## Covered Items:
- Broken zippers, torn seams due to defective stitching, defective hardware or straps.

## Exclusions:
- Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, chemical spills, unauthorized modifications, or cosmetic abrasions.

## Warranty Claim Process:
Submit proof of purchase, a description of the defect, and 2-3 photos to warranty@example.com. Approved claims receive a repair, direct product replacement, or store credit equal to the purchase value.
